ORIGIN STORY

It started with a bear named Gary who found a honeycomb so perfect he refused to eat it. Instead he built it a shrine out of river stones. Word spread. More bears came. Some brought salmon as offerings. Nobody remembers who declared it a cult, but by the third gathering there were rules, a chant, and a guy in charge of snacks.

Today the Grizzly Cult numbers exactly 199 — no more, no less, because the forest only fits 199 good hiding spots. Each bear holds a rank, a grudge against squirrels, and a small non-transferable piece of the shrine.

This is not a metaphor for anything. It is just bears, being extremely serious about a sandwich.

THE SACRED LAWS

Rules Carved Into The Shrine

I

Thou Shalt Nap First, Ask Questions Later

All cult business is conducted after a mandatory nap. Meetings called before noon are automatically postponed.

II

The Honeycomb Is Not For Eating

It is a symbol. Symbols are sacred. Symbols are also, frankly, extremely tempting, which is the whole test.

III

Never Trust A Bear Without a Salmon

Ownership of a Grizzly Cult NFT is proof of salmon. No salmon, no vote at Cave Council.

IV

199 Is The Number. There Is No Second Batch.

The forest has 199 hiding spots. We are not cutting down trees to make room for more. That would defeat the point.
